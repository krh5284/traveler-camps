## How Traveler Camps works

- **Search:** use the [campground finder](campgrounds/) to look by name, city, state or distance from a town.
- **Compare:** every listing shows whether it has full hookups, 50 amp electric service and year-round availability, and whether other travelers have verified it.
- **Contribute:** know a campground that welcomes long stays? [Add it to the map](new-campground/).
