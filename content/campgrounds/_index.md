## What the labels mean

- **User verified:** a traveling worker has confirmed the listing.
- **Full hookups:** water, electric and sewer are available at the site.
- **50 amp electric:** the campground offers 50 amp electric service.
- **Open year-round:** the campground stays open all twelve months.

Rates, availability and long-term policies change, so always confirm details with the campground before you book.
